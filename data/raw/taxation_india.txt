Taxation in India depends on the type of income and investment.

Q: What is capital gains tax?
Capital gains tax is levied on profits earned from selling assets such as
stocks, mutual funds, or property.

Q: What is Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG)?
STCG on equity shares sold within one year is taxed at 15%.

Q: What is Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG)?
LTCG on equity shares held for more than one year is taxed at 10% on gains
above ₹1 lakh.

Q: What is Section 80C?
Section 80C allows tax deductions up to ₹1.5 lakh on eligible investments
such as ELSS, PPF, EPF, and life insurance premiums.

Q: What is Section 80D?
Section 80D provides deductions for health insurance premiums.

Q: Are tax laws fixed?
No, tax laws may change depending on government policies.
