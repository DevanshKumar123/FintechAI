Investment refers to allocating money with the expectation of generating
future income or appreciation over time.

Q: What is investment?
Investment is the process of putting money into assets such as stocks,
mutual funds, fixed deposits, bonds, gold, or real estate to grow wealth.

Q: Why should people invest?
Investing helps beat inflation, grow long-term wealth, and achieve financial
goals such as retirement, education, or buying a home.

Q: What are common investment options?
Common options include Fixed Deposits (FD), Mutual Funds, Stocks, Bonds,
Gold, Public Provident Fund (PPF), National Pension System (NPS),
and Real Estate.

Q: What is risk in investment?
Risk refers to the possibility of losing money or earning lower-than-expected
returns. Higher returns usually involve higher risk.

Q: How should beginners start investing?
Beginners should start with understanding financial goals, building an
emergency fund, and investing in low-risk or diversified instruments such
as mutual funds or SIPs.

Q: What is diversification?
Diversification means spreading investments across different asset classes
to reduce overall risk.
