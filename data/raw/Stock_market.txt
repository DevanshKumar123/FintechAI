The stock market allows investors to buy and sell shares of publicly listed
companies. Stock prices fluctuate based on demand, supply, company
performance, and economic conditions.

Q: What is the stock market?
The stock market is a platform where shares of companies are traded.

Q: Why do stock prices fluctuate?
Stock prices change due to company earnings, economic data, interest rates,
inflation, global events, and investor sentiment.

Q: What is long-term investing?
Long-term investing involves holding stocks for several years to benefit
from compounding and business growth.

Q: Is the stock market risky?
The stock market is volatile in the short term but long-term investing
generally reduces risk.

Q: What is market volatility?
Volatility refers to rapid price movements in the stock market over a short
period.

Q: What are blue-chip stocks?
Blue-chip stocks are shares of financially stable and well-established
companies with a strong reputation.

Q: What is market correction?
A market correction refers to a temporary decline in stock prices.
